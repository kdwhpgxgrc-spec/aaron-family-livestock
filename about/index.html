Who Is Aaron Family Livestock?

Aaron Family Livestock LLC is a family registered Angus operation in Gladewater, Texas. The program traces its identity to 2005, the year Vance was born and the Aaron family became complete, and now focuses on AI sire genetics, embryo calves and foundation females.

The Program Today

Family Attention, Deliberate Matings

The Aaron family works with established cattle programs, including Circle S Farms, to source genetics that fit its goals. The current program includes Moxy 406 AI influence and Circle S 5707 × Colburn Primo 5153 embryos due in early 2027.

Every animal receives direct family attention. The goal is to develop cattle that can serve competitive families while building a useful registered Angus cow herd for the future.
