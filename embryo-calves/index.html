5707 × Primo Embryo Calves

Direct Answer

When Will the AFL Embryo Calves Be Born?

The first Circle S 5707 × Colburn Primo 5153 embryo calves in the Aaron Family Livestock program are due in early 2027. Availability will depend on the resulting calf crop and individual development.

The Mating

Maternal Power Meets a Proven Show Sire

Circle S 5707 brings the maternal side of the mating. Colburn Primo 5153 contributes a widely recognized Angus show-cattle pedigree. AFL selected the pairing as the launch point for its embryo program.

  • DonorCircle S 5707
  • SireColburn Primo 5153 · AAA registration 18217480
  • SourceEmbryos purchased through Circle S Farms
  • ExpectedEarly 2027

What Happens Next?

Each calf will be evaluated as it develops. AFL will assess structure, balance, phenotype and fit for the program before making availability decisions.

Can Families Reserve One?

Contact Bryan or Vance Aaron to express interest and receive updates. The site does not promise reservations before the calf crop is born and evaluated.

Where Are They Raised?

The Aaron Family Livestock program is based in Gladewater, Texas, in the East Texas region.
