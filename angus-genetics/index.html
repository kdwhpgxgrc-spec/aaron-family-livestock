What Defines the AFL Genetics Program?

The program combines registered Angus females with proven sire lines and targeted embryo matings. Each mating is selected around documented pedigree, maternal quality, structural balance and the kind of look required in competitive show cattle.

AI Sires

The current program includes Circle S Moxy 406 and genetics connected to Silveiras Style 9303, selected to add proven show-ring and maternal influence.

Embryo Program

Aaron Family Livestock purchased Circle S 5707 × Colburn Primo 5153 embryos from Circle S Farms. The first calves are due in early 2027.

Foundation Females

The cow herd includes an El Jefe influence and a registered Phat Amy daughter by Silveiras Style 9303, AFL's first registered Angus female.

How We Evaluate a Mating

More Than a Famous Name

A sire's record matters, but the mating must also complement the female. AFL studies pedigree, performance information, maternal strength and phenotype before deciding how a sire fits the program.

  • PedigreeHow the sire and female families complement one another.
  • Maternal ValueThe long-term usefulness of daughters retained in the herd.
  • Show-Ring FunctionBalance, structure and presence suited to competitive families.
