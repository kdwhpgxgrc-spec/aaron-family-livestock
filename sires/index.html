Which Sires Influence Aaron Family Livestock?

AFL's current genetic lineup includes Circle S Moxy 406, Colburn Primo 5153, Silveiras Style 9303 and El Jefe influence, supported by Circle S 5707 and the program's Phat Amy daughter.

Circle S Moxy 406

AI sire by PVF Marvel 9185 and out of Seldom Rest Bardot 6092. The AFL foundation female is bred to Moxy 406.

Colburn Primo 5153

Embryo sire used on Circle S 5707. The first calves from this AFL embryo mating are due in early 2027.

Silveiras Style 9303

Sire of AFL's Phat Amy daughter, the first registered Angus female and a foundation of the cow herd.

El Jefe

The AFL cow herd includes El Jefe daughters, adding maternal depth to the registered Angus program.

Circle S 5707

Embryo donor in AFL's first purchased flush, paired with Colburn Primo 5153 through Circle S Farms.

Phat Amy Daughter

A registered Angus female by Silveiras Style 9303 and the foundation female now bred to Moxy 406 AI.
