A Family Built on Cattle

Aaron Family Livestock is a registered Angus operation rooted in East Texas. EST. 2005 — the year Vance was born and the Aaron family became complete. Every animal we produce carries the same purpose: breed the kind of genetics that show families trust and champions are made from.

We've been blessed to work alongside elite programs like Circle S Farms, sourcing AI sires and embryo genetics that give our calves every advantage in the ring. Our first 5707 × Primo 5153 embryo calves are due in 2026 — and we believe they'll be something special.

This is a family program. Every animal gets our full attention. That's the Aaron way.
