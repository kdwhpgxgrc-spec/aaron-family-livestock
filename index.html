Registered Angus Show Cattle FAQ

About the Program

What does Aaron Family Livestock raise?

Aaron Family Livestock raises registered Angus show cattle, AI sire genetics, embryo calves, and foundation females from a family-run program in Gladewater, Texas.

Where is Aaron Family Livestock located?

The program is based in Gladewater, Texas and serves show cattle families across East Texas and beyond.

Which genetics are featured?

Featured genetics include Circle S Moxy 406, Colburn Primo 5153, El Jefe, Silveiras Style 9303, Circle S 5707, and Phat Amy daughter foundation females.
